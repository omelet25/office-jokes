# Office Jokes — candidates

Task: one line about office life, under 200 characters (gate: entry.txt
must be 1–199 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace). The judge weighs,
in order: a genuine joke about office life; an unexpected turn; ringing
true about the office; tight wording; a fresh take.

## Drafts

1. The printer has been "processing" my report for three hours — it is
   reading it more carefully than my manager ever will.
   - True and has a turn, but "processing" gags are well worn.

2. Our printer only jams when the document actually matters, which makes
   it the most honest performance review in the building.
   - Setup everyone has lived (the jam always lands on the important
     print job), then a real turn: the printer is re-cast as the one
     honest reviewer in the office. Tight, fresh angle.

3. I scheduled a meeting to reduce our meetings. It was so productive it
   now recurs weekly.
   - Very tight and true, but the meeting-about-meetings premise is a
     familiar one.

4. I put "per my last email" in 47-point font. HR called it a threat;
   I call it an accessibility feature.
   - Fresh and specific; slightly longer fuse before the laugh.

5. Our standup is capped at fifteen minutes, which is remarkable, because
   no one on this team has sat down since 2019.
   - Good turn, mid freshness.

6. My out-of-office reply says I'll answer when I'm back; my calendar
   says I'm back in a meeting; the meeting invites my out-of-office reply.
   - Neat loop, but the longest and loosest of the set.

7. The reply-all button has ended more careers than any performance
   review in company history.
   - Punchy, but reads a little like an observation rather than a joke
     with a turn.

## Pick

Candidate 2 — the printer as the building's most honest performance
reviewer. It is a genuine joke (setup, then re-framing), it rings true
for anyone who has ever printed something urgent, the wording is tight,
and the angle is fresh.

Final polish pass: keep "only jams when the document actually matters"
as the setup and "the most honest performance review in the building"
as the landing. Final line (124 characters):

Our printer only jams when the document actually matters, which makes it the most honest performance review in the building.
